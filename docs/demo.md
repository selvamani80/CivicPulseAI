# College & Pilot Demonstration Manual

1. Open the application.
2. In the **Header**, notice the live signal ticker displaying active warning lead time (3.8 Hours) and risk counts.
3. On the **Landing Overview**, go to the **Live Environmental & Signal Risk Simulator**.
4. Drag **Rainfall Intensity** to 75 mm/hr, **Citizen Reports** to 18, and **Drainage Obstruction** to 80%.
5. Click **Execute Predictive Calculation**. Note how the AI predicts an 89% Waterlogging Risk for Ward 172 (Velachery) with a 3-6 hour expected window.
6. Click **Officer Map & Risks** to view the geospatial prediction map.
7. Click the red marker on Velachery 100ft Road to inspect the SHAP factors and recommended advisory actions.
8. Click **Confirm & Record Preventive Action** to simulate field officer dispatch.
9. Switch to **Citizen Portal** and test **Report by Voice** in Tanglish or Tamil ("Velachery main road la water nikkuthu").
10. Open **AI Analytics** to review Precision (91.4%), Recall (88.2%), and Model Drift status.
