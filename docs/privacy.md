# Data Privacy & Ethical AI Standards

1. **Strict Non-Profiling Rule:**
   - CivicPulse AI never tracks individual citizens or creates "citizen trust scores."
   - Anonymous reporting automatically redacts PII.
2. **Geospatial Privacy:**
   - Public maps cluster citizen signals into 300m density areas to protect exact residence identities.
3. **No Biometric Surveillance:**
   - Computer vision checks strictly analyze physical infrastructure—never human faces or license plates.
